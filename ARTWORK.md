# Artwork and fonts

**Frieren** is an unofficial fan theme featuring an AI-generated illustration of the character from *Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End*. The character and series belong to their respective rights holders. The theme configuration, selection overlays and renderer were prepared for BetterGRUB. The project license covers its software and theme definitions. This repository grants no rights to the underlying character.

**Binary Stars** is original procedural artwork. Its generator and image are available under GPL-3.0-or-later. To regenerate the PNG, run `python3 tools/draw_binary_stars.py` with Pillow installed.

**Drakengard 3** and **NieR: Automata** are unofficial fan themes with AI-generated illustrations of Zero and 2B. The project created the illustrations and boot-menu layouts for these examples. The characters, game titles and related marks belong to their respective owners.

**Minecraft** uses Lxtharia and contributors' Minegrub title and world-selection artwork. The two-screen interaction comes from their double-menu project. The bundled adaptation credits the original revisions and includes both MIT notices. Its menu typography uses 22 px and 30 px subsets of Idrees Hassan's Monocraft v4.2.1 under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. [Minegrub sources and notices](examples/minecraft/NOTICE.txt) · [Double Minegrub Menu](https://github.com/Lxtharia/double-minegrub-menu) · [Monocraft license](examples/minecraft/LICENSE-Monocraft.txt)

The two PF2 menu fonts are subsets of DejaVu Sans. Their redistribution terms and attribution are in [the DejaVu font license](examples/fonts/FONT-LICENSE.txt). In the EFI archive this notice is in `grub/fonts/FONT-LICENSE.txt`.
