# Artwork and fonts

**Frieren** is an unofficial fan theme featuring an AI-generated illustration of the character from *Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End*. The character and series belong to their respective rights holders. The theme configuration, selection overlays and renderer were prepared for BetterGRUB. The project license covers its software and theme definitions. This repository grants no rights to the underlying character.

**Binary Stars** is original procedural artwork. Its generator and image are available under GPL-3.0-or-later. To regenerate the PNG, run `python3 tools/draw_binary_stars.py` with Pillow installed.

The two PF2 menu fonts are subsets of DejaVu Sans. Their redistribution terms and attribution are in [the DejaVu font license](examples/fonts/FONT-LICENSE.txt). In the EFI archive this notice is in `grub/fonts/FONT-LICENSE.txt`.
